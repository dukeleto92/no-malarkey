# No Malarkey

## Electoral map targets (v0.10)

**This section is the authoritative source for map work.** When adjusting
`candidate_state_multiplier_json` (Code 2) or reasoning about where the map
should land, these targets govern — not the current computed margins, and not
the 2020 real-world result. Where a measured margin and a target below
disagree, the target is what the map is wrong against.

Targets are **margins at a national environment of D+2**.

delivery-to-polling coefficient. States at or beyond R+8 are treated as out of
reach for now, but that threshold may move once the coefficient is fixed —
so do not harden anything against it.
